How is this judged?

Outdated = 10 or more commits behind the upstream default branch, or behind and not bumped for 90 days. Behind = 1 to 9 behind, bumped within 90 days. Current = at the default-branch head. Unpushed = the pinned commit is on no host. Not measured = the commits behind could not be counted (the reason is in the details).

A configuration is judged on its direct pins (its worst one). A pin row shows the worst state at or beneath it. Days = since anyone last moved the pin: Fresh under 30 d, Aging 30 to 89 d, Stale 90 d or more.

A frozen silicon configuration is old on purpose: it reads Frozen, says what it would be if live, and is never counted as outdated. ? = a guessed characterisation. [brackets] = not known yet. Pages read stored scans only.
